# Repeating Knot Cards

**[Download Knot-Cards.pdf](Knot-Cards.pdf)**: Repeating Knot Cards, a pack of 52 Celtic knot cards to print and cut out.

Every card is a little patch of knotwork, and every one can be turned any way: wherever it goes, the ribbons
still pass alternately over and under. Lay four cards in a square, repeat the square, and the knot takes on one
of nine kinds of symmetry: nine of the seventeen wallpaper groups.

## What is in the PDF

1. The instruction sheet: the four kinds of card and a recipe for each of the nine groups.
2. Four sheets of 12 pattern cards (48 in all: 16 T, 16 G, 8 R, 8 L).
3. Four reference cards: rules, targets, and questions to wonder about.
4. Two box wraps, front and bottom.

## Printing

- A4, single-sided, at 100% ("actual size", not "fit to page").
- Heavy card, 300 gsm or more. Cards are 60 mm square; cut on the crop marks.
- Box wraps: each is 140 mm square, a 60 mm face with a 40 mm border to fold round the sides. The ticks in the
  margins mark the fold lines.
