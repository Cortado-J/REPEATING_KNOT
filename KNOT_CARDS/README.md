# Repeating Knot Cards

**[Download Knot-Cards.pdf](Knot-Cards.pdf)**: Repeating Knot Cards, a pack of 60 Celtic knot cards to print and cut out.

Every card is a little patch of knotwork, and every one can be turned any way: wherever it goes, the ribbons
still pass alternately over and under. Lay four cards in a square, repeat the square, and the knot takes on one
of nine kinds of symmetry: nine of the seventeen wallpaper groups.

Each of the nine target cards has a recipe on the front. Build its pattern, work out its group, then turn the
card over to check: the back marks the pattern's mirrors, glides and turn centres and names the group.

## What is in the PDF

1. The instruction sheet: the four kinds of card, how to play, and the nine targets with their groups.
2. Pages 2–5: four sheets of 12 pattern cards (48 in all: 16 T, 16 G, 8 R, 8 E).
3. Pages 6–7: nine target cards and three reference cards (rules, how to read the backs, questions to wonder
   about), fronts on page 6 and backs on page 7.
4. Page 8: two box wraps, front and bottom.

## Printing

- A4, at 100% ("actual size", not "fit to page"). Single-sided, except pages 6 and 7: print those two on one
  sheet, double-sided, flipping on the long edge.
- Heavy card, 300 gsm or more. Cards are 60 mm square; cut on the crop marks.
- Box wraps: each is 140 mm square, a 60 mm face with a 40 mm border to fold round the sides. The ticks in the
  margins mark the fold lines.
